Plastic Waste Into STEM Education

Plasycle turns plastic waste into 3D printer filament, then into STEM learning aids for rural schools that can't afford them. B40 families earn income. Children get science equipment. Plastic stays out of landfills.

Try the Demo Learn More

The Problem

Malaysia generates 15.38 million tonnes of solid waste every year, with over 1 million tonnes being plastic. Meanwhile, rural schools lack basic science equipment — microscopes, lab tools, learning aids.

Most plastic ends up in landfills or oceans. But plastic is a resource — it can be shredded, melted, and turned into 3D printer filament. That filament can print STEM learning aids that rural schools desperately need. The loop solves two problems at once.

Our Solution

Plasycle combines AI plastic identification with 3D printing to create STEM learning aids for rural schools.

🖨️

How the Loop Works

Plasycle creates a closed-loop circular economy — from plastic waste to STEM education.

1️⃣

Collect

B40 families collect plastic waste from their homes and neighborhoods.

2️⃣

Scan

Plasycle's AI identifies the plastic type and shows its filament potential.

3️⃣

Convert

Plastic is shredded, melted, and extruded into 3D printer filament.

4️⃣

Print

Filament is 3D-printed into STEM learning aids for rural schools.

5️⃣

Deliver

Aids are donated to schools that can't afford them.

6️⃣

Earn

Families earn income from plastic collection. The loop continues.
