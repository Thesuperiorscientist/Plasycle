About Plasycle

A social business project aligned with the Three Zeros framework.

Problem Statement

Malaysia generates over 1 million tonnes of plastic waste annually, most of which ends up in landfills or oceans. Meanwhile, thousands of rural schools lack basic STEM learning aids and science equipment. Plasycle is a free, AI-powered platform that identifies plastic types, calculates 3D filament yield, and shows how plastic waste can be turned into STEM learning aids for rural schools — empowering B40 families to earn from plastic waste while educating the next generation.

Target Users

Our primary beneficiaries are B40 low-income families in Malaysia, especially those in PPR housing and urban poor communities. They generate plastic waste daily and can earn income by collecting it for Plasycle.

Our secondary beneficiaries are rural school students who lack STEM learning aids. Every kg of plastic Plasycle collects becomes 5 STEM learning kits — reaching 2,500 rural students per kg.

Plastic Types We Identify

PET (Type 1)

Water bottles, soda bottles. High-quality filament. Strong and clear.

PE (Type 2 & 4)

Milk jugs, detergent bottles, plastic bags. Flexible and durable.

PC (Type 7)

Baby bottles, water gallons. Very high quality, impact-resistant.

PP (Type 5)

Yogurt cups, bottle caps, containers. Heat-resistant.

PS (Type 6)

Styrofoam, disposable cups. Limited filament use.

Others

Mixed or unknown plastic. Not suitable for filament.

What We Make From Plastic

Every plastic type becomes a specific STEM learning aid for rural schools.

PET → Phone Microscope

Turns any phone into a microscope. Reaches 500+ students per kit.

PET → Water Filtration Kit

Teaches filtration and water science. Reaches 300+ students per kit.

PE → Simple Machines Kit

Levers, pulleys, gears for physics. Reaches 350+ students per kit.

PC → Beaker Holder Set

Safe chemistry lab equipment. Reaches 200+ students per set.

PP → Math Fraction Tiles

Visual math learning. Reaches 500+ students per set.

PS → Solar System Model

Astronomy teaching aid. Reaches 400+ students per model.

Alternative Solutions Considered

Traditional Plastic Recycling

Rejected because: Low value per kg, no education impact, families earn little.

Donation-Based School Aid

Rejected because: Not sustainable, dependent on charity, no plastic reduction.

Commercial 3D Printing Business

Rejected because: Profit-focused, doesn't reach rural schools, no social mission.

Why We Chose Plasycle

  • Closed-loop economy — plastic waste becomes STEM education, creating double impact.
  • AI-powered identification — instant plastic type detection removes the knowledge barrier.
  • Multiple beneficiaries — B40 families earn, rural students learn, environment wins.
  • Empowers dignity — no public assistance, families use their own phones.
  • Scalable — runs in the browser, deployed on Netlify, no server costs.
  • Delivers on all Three Zeros — poverty, unemployment, carbon.

Social Business Model

Revenue Stream Who Pays Purpose
Free app for B40 families No one Core mission — plastic collection
STEM aid sales to NGOs & schools NGOs, private schools, universities Main revenue — funds free aid for rural schools
Filament sales to makers 3D printing enthusiasts Additional revenue
CSR sponsorships Corporations (RM10,000/year) Subsidizes free aids for rural schools

Self-sustaining model: The app remains free for B40 families. Revenue from selling STEM aids to NGOs and private schools funds free aids for rural schools, plastic collection, and the app itself.
